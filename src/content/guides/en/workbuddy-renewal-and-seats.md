## The short answer

Renewal is more than paying again. A month before expiry, **pull usage, check seat counts and confirm the edition still fits**, then decide whether to renew, adjust or stop. An Enterprise authorization stops working at expiry, so plan ahead to avoid interrupting the team.

Compiled from the WorkBuddy and Tencent Cloud official documentation, checked on 2026-10-05. Rules can change; follow the official pages when you act.

## When this applies

For team leads and IT administrators already using WorkBuddy who face expiry or need to change seats. For a first purchase, start with [what to confirm before buying](../workbuddy-procurement-checklist/).

## Personal plans: renewal, upgrades and refunds

From the official plan-management page:

| Item                | Official rule                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Auto-renewal        | On by default for continuous monthly and annual plans; turn it off on the website’s plans-and-usage page or in WeChat Pay settings |
| When to turn it off | At least five working days before the current cycle ends                                                                           |
| Manual renewal      | Month by month; remaining plus new time may not exceed 30 months                                                                   |
| Upgrades            | Upgrades allowed, downgrades not; the remaining value is credited                                                                  |
| Refunds             | Within 24 hours for monthly or 7 calendar days for annual, with no credits used; once per order                                    |
| Credits             | Granted monthly, valid that month, no roll-over                                                                                    |

With no roll-over and no downgrades, “buy high and step down later” does not work. Check real usage before choosing an edition.

## Enterprise: expiry and seat changes

WorkBuddy Enterprise is subscribed per seat, and administrators assign seats in the console. Plan for:

1. **Expiry**: the official credits page says Enterprise cannot be used at all after expiry. If renewal approval takes time, start at least a month ahead.
2. **Seat changes**: how and when seats are added or removed, and how they are billed, follow Tencent Cloud’s order rules and the formal quote. Check for long-unused seats first.
3. **Reclaiming seats**: when staff leave or move, reclaim their seats promptly and reassign them.
4. **Shared credits**: Flagship and Exclusive grant credits per seat, shared in the team and refreshed monthly. If usage concentrates on a few people, watch the team total.
5. **Edition thresholds**: the official page lists a 100-seat minimum for Exclusive; if headcount changes a lot, confirm you still meet the edition’s conditions.

## Invoices

From the official invoice page:

- **Individual users** request invoices in the WorkBuddy personal center with header type, invoice title, taxpayer ID and other details; invoices usually arrive by email within 24 hours.
- **Enterprise users** handle invoicing in the enterprise backend of the Tencent Cloud console.

When buying through a reseller, who issues the invoice and how follows the order agreement — confirm before ordering.

## Pre-renewal review

A month before expiry, check:

1. **Usage**: credits per person over the past three months — who runs out, who barely uses it.
2. **Seats**: active users versus authorised seats; departed staff’s seats reclaimed.
3. **Edition**: whether usage or administration needs call for an upgrade, or some users could move to another edition.
4. **Term**: monthly or annual; annual is usually discounted but less flexible.
5. **Budget and approval**: renewal amount, payment method and time needed for internal approval.
6. **Invoice and contract**: whether invoice title, tax ID or other details have changed.

Put the review next to the partner’s quote before choosing a renewal plan.

## Common mistakes

- **Forgetting auto-renewal**: continuous plans renew by default; turn it off in time if you do not want to renew.
- **Acting on the expiry date**: Enterprise stops working at expiry, and late approval halts the team.
- **Not reclaiming seats**: departed staff hold seats while new colleagues go without.
- **Renewing by headcount without looking at usage**: when some run out and others barely use it, reallocating beats adding seats.

## FAQ

### Is our data kept after expiry?

The pages above do not state post-expiry data retention. Export anything you need before expiry and confirm in writing with the official team or your partner.

### Can we reduce seats mid-term?

Enterprise seat changes follow Tencent Cloud’s order rules and the formal quote. Personal plans cannot be downgraded.

### Is the renewal price the same as the first purchase?

Not necessarily. Prices, discounts and promotions change; use the official price or partner quote at renewal.

### Can Easy AI send renewal reminders?

Whether reminders are provided, and by whom, can be confirmed and written into the order.
