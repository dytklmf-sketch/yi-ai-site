## The short answer

The most useful starting point is **a verifiable resource description and responsibility list**, not just a price. Explain what you can supply, the basis for doing so, relevant limitations and who handles usage, incidents and settlement.

Easy AI discusses model services and direct supply-channel cooperation. A direct relationship with a channel does not imply direct supply from a model developer. This guide does not invite transfers of unknown-origin accounts or keys, or circumvention of platform limits.

## When this applies

This checklist is for partners entitled to offer the resources concerned and buyers establishing a verification process. Resolve doubts about supply permissions and data responsibilities first. A small paid test does not substitute for authorization.

## Six items for the first discussion

1. **Role:** The contact's responsibilities and whether the resource holder and service provider are the same party.
2. **Scope:** Model identifiers, interface format, input/output types, service regions and supply period. Label unconfirmed items.
3. **Permissions:** Verifiable rights to use and supply the resources, and evidence that can be reviewed under appropriate confidentiality arrangements.
4. **Constraints:** Request rate, token limits, concurrency and planned maintenance windows. Describe conditions rather than only an aggregate volume.
5. **Settlement:** Billing units, periods, sample reconciliation, charge disputes and notice of price changes.
6. **Responsibilities and exit:** Incident contacts, data and log handling, suspension conditions, unfinished tasks and unused balances.

The official rate-limit reference helps distinguish aggregate allowance from throughput over time. Actual supply limits must be confirmed with the provider concerned.

## Validate in the right order

Verify identity, permissions and scope first. Agree on a bounded test's objective, cost, data and request frequency. Use approved samples and isolated test credentials, not production keys in ordinary messages or documents.

After testing, summarize compatibility, errors and measurement discrepancies. Separate resolved questions from pending ones before discussing scale and formal settlement. A successful test does not establish long-term capacity or a response commitment.

## Common questions

### Is the lowest price sufficient?

No. Supply permissions, availability, responsibilities and settlement must also be clear before proposals can be compared.

### Must the supplier be the model developer?

Describe the channel role accurately. Easy AI does not present channel cooperation as an unverified direct relationship with a model developer.

### Should we send account credentials first?

No. Start with a credential-free resource brief. If validation becomes necessary, agree on controlled access, permissions, expiry and revocation.
