## The short answer

Before purchasing WorkBuddy, specify **who will use it, for which tasks, which edition you need, for how long, and who owns follow-up responsibilities**. Compare proposals against the same brief instead of asking only for a discount. Easy AI offers reseller procurement consulting. This is an inquiry checklist, not a statement of product entitlements or a quotation.

## When this applies

Use this guide for a first team purchase, additional purchasing for existing accounts, or a comparison of procurement options. Individual trial experience does not establish team authorization rights. For existing accounts, explain ownership and current usage.

If you have not selected a product, start with two or three real tasks. Do not assume a particular deployment, administration or integration feature is available.

## The procurement checklist

1. **Users:** Team roles, intended headcount, pilot participants and eventual users.
2. **Tasks:** Input materials, required outputs and steps that still need human review.
3. **Product and authorization:** Exact edition name, term, account ownership, quantity and permitted scope.
4. **Environment:** Operating systems, network constraints, existing accounts and internal approval requirements for sensitive material.
5. **Quotation basis:** Quantity and period covered, tax treatment, renewal conditions and possible additional charges.
6. **Delivery and responsibility:** Target date, evidence of delivery, incident contacts and ownership of services beyond procurement.

Mark each item as confirmed, pending or not needed. Record the confirmation date and evidence. Ask about unresolved items rather than treating ambiguous promotional wording as an agreed term.

## A practical process

Send the team size, task description and intended timeline. Align the edition, quantity, period and quotation conditions. Then confirm the order and delivery arrangements. If a trial or technical validation is needed, check availability, allowances and cost first; do not assume it is free.

List deployment, training, technical support and after-sales arrangements separately. Procurement consulting does not mean they are included, or imply a particular authorization tier for Easy AI.

## Common questions

### Can headcount alone determine the price?

No. Edition, duration, order arrangements and separately agreed services also matter. An estimate is not a final quotation until the scope is confirmed.

### Are all advertised features included?

Check the official description for the intended edition against the actual proposal. Retain the version and date of the documentation. Resolve conflicting descriptions before purchasing.

### Can we inquire without a full brief?

Yes. One representative task, a headcount range and a target start date are enough to begin. Do not send passwords, API keys or unapproved raw business data in an initial inquiry.
